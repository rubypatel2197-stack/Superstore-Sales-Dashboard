## 🌟 Project Overview

The **Superstore Sales Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. It provides meaningful insights into sales performance, profitability, customer segments, and product analysis through interactive charts and key performance indicators (KPIs).

The dashboard consists of two pages:

- 📈 **Page 1: Superstore Sales**
- 💰 **Page 2: Profit & Product Analysis**

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- ⭐ Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- ⭐ Monitor total quantity and order count.
- ⭐ Compare sales across different cities and regions.
- ⭐ Understand customer segment contributions.
- ⭐ Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
- ⭐ Identify profitable product categories and sub-categories.
- ⭐ Support data-driven business decisions.

- 🏙️ New York City has the highest sales among the cities displayed.
- 👥 The Consumer segment contributes the largest share of sales.
- 📈 Sales reach their highest displayed level in November.
- 💻 Category-wise analysis helps compare Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
- 💰 Sub-category profit analysis helps identify stronger and weaker product areas.

## 🚀 How to Use the Dashboard

1. 📥 Download or clone the project repository.
2. 💻 Open the `.pbix` file using Power BI Desktop.
3. 📊 Explore the Superstore Sales page.
4. 🔍 Use the Region and City slicers to filter results.
5. 📈 Navigate to Profit & Product Analysis.
6. 🖱️ Interact with charts to explore related data.
